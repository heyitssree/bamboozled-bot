### Found (not fixed)
Full list with test references is in `docs/rules-as-implemented.md` section 4. The ones that change behaviour:
1. AFK kick skips the next player's turn and jumps the round counter.
2. FFF: quitters and AFK-kicked players vanish from the final results.
3. Double Down / tiebreak questions ignore laws and the Sombrero.
4. Mist "2 turns" is effectively "rest of this turn plus one".
5. Wango Again resets the chain depth; Golden Pass only guards the post-wrong-answer draw.
6. Survival counts a fetch error as a personal best.
